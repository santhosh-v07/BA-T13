# Superstore Business Analysis

## Objective
Analyze the Superstore dataset using Tableau Public to identify meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations, comparisons, trends, and relationships.

## Student Instructions
- Explore the dataset to identify important patterns, trends, comparisons, and relationships.
- Create a dashboard containing different visualizations.
- Use adjustable filters to allow users to interact with and explore the dashboard.
- Select visualizations that best explain the findings.
- Present key findings, a conclusion, suggestions, and recommendations based on the analysis.

## Key Findings
- Sales and profitability should be evaluated together, since high sales do not always translate into high profit.
- Performance can vary across product categories, sub-categories, customer segments, and regions.
- Comparing results across these business dimensions helps identify stronger areas and those that may need attention.
- Interactive filters allow users to investigate specific parts of the business and examine how performance changes across selections.

## Conclusion
The Superstore analysis provides an interactive view of business performance and supports a more informed understanding of sales and profitability across different dimensions. Reviewing the measures together helps distinguish revenue growth from profitable performance.

## Suggestions
- Review products or sub-categories with weak profitability to understand the causes.
- Examine discounting and pricing practices where profit is low relative to sales.
- Compare regional and customer-segment performance to identify areas that may require operational or sales improvements.
- Continue monitoring performance over time to identify changing patterns.

## Recommendations
- Prioritize decisions using both sales and profit measures rather than sales alone.
- Investigate low-profit areas before changing prices, discounts, product mix, or sales strategy.
- Use the dashboard filters to validate patterns for specific regions, categories, sub-categories, and customer segments before taking action.
- Revisit the dashboard regularly as new data becomes available.
